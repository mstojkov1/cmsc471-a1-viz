Admission Rate for Elite Colleges

Across 139 colleges in the United States, children from richer families attend at a far higher rate in comparison to those less fortunate.

About this visualization

Dataset

For our project, we selected to review College Admissions data from Raj Chetty's research at Harvard. Our data contains 139 colleges with 14 parent income groups listed per college. We decided to take about 9 of the 81 possible columns focusing on relative attendance, relative application, and attendance if applied.

Design decisions and interactions

To display our data, we used a line chart to show how admission and application rates differ from poor families to wealthier families, using 1 line per college. We also use a scatter plot to compare each college's bottom 20% to its top 1% in one done, using a diagonal line to show which colleges favor wealthier families.

References

We used data from the Opportunity Insights data page, as well as D3.js for visuals and brush features.
